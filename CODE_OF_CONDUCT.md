# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, technically rigorous community.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, licensing, and responsible-disclosure boundaries.
- Acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes transparently.
- Use synthetic or properly redacted examples.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of sensitive data, sustained disruption, and retaliation are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through an appropriate private GitHub channel. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants. They will seek to protect reporter privacy while providing fair review.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, release channels, and project-related community interactions.
